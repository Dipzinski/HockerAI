How leads are scored

CriterionPointsFrom the OSHA records
Industry fit0-3NAICS code: woodworking and metal fabrication 3; minerals and food 2; pharma, plastics, foundries 1
Dust evidence0-33 dust-specific citation (silica, ventilation, air contaminants, metal dust, grain); 2 related citation (housekeeping, respirators, spray booths) or a dust-program inspection; 1 other serious citation
Recency0-3How recent the dust evidence is: within 12, 24, or 36 months
Site size0-2Employees at the inspected site: 20-500 earns 2; 10-19 or 501-1,000 earns 1

Hot is 8-11 and Warm is 5-7. A facility with no citation or dust-program inspection in the last 36 months is not a lead, whatever its fit. Michigan's state plan (MIOSHA) cites its own rules, which are mapped to the same categories.
