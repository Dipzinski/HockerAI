Hocker North America ICP & Scoring Rubric

The 0–12 point scoring model combines federal OSHA enforcement data, Michigan state-plan (MIOSHA) citations, and EPA Toxics Release Inventory (TRI) metal air emissions to identify manufacturers with an immediate commercial need for industrial dust collection and extraction systems.

Criterion Points Scoring Logic & Evidence Sources
Industry Fit 0–3 pts Priority NAICS codes: Woodworking/Cabinetry & Metal Fabrication (3 pts); Cement/Minerals & Food Processing (2 pts); Pharma, Plastics & Foundries (1 pt).
Dust Evidence 0–3 pts 3 pts: Dust-specific citation (silica, industrial ventilation, air-contaminant limits, lead/metal dust) or General Duty citation during a Combustible Dust NEP.
2 pts: Dust-related citation (housekeeping 1910.22, spray booths, respirators) or inspection under a dust NEP.
1 pt: Any other serious OSHA citation.
Recency 0–3 pts Newest dust evidence within 12 months (3 pts), 24 months (2 pts), or 36 months (1 pt). Fresh citations carry impending abatement deadlines.
Site Size 0–2 pts Headcount at the inspected facility (not company-wide): 20–500 employees (2 pts); 10–19 or 501–1,000 employees (1 pt).
TRI Metal Air Release 0–1 pt EPA TRI 2024 Form R reporting a positive fugitive or stack air release for a metal compound, matched to an OSHA facility by normalized name, ZIP, and state.

Compliance & Safety Hold Rules

A high score never overrides a safety hold. Outreach is strictly blocked for human review if a facility has:

  • A workplace fatality inspection in the last 36 months
  • A serious-injury/catastrophe inspection in the last 24 months
  • Any citation currently under active administrative contest

Outreach Guardrails & Verification

Hocker North America sales reps use AI-drafted briefs as starting points only. Messages must cite public records neutrally (allegations until final), never guarantee compliance or quote prices, and focus on consultative engineering support.
